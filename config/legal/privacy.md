# Privacy Policy

Draft. This text must be reviewed and replaced by the client's legal team before launch.

## What we store

The application stores the user accounts created by your administrator (username, display name and an encrypted password), the factories and machines your administrator adds, the Excel files uploaded for each working day, and a record of who changed what and when.

## Why we store it

This information is used only to show factory and machine status to the people your administrator has given access to.

## Who can see it

Only signed-in users can see data, and each user sees only the factories assigned to them. Administrators can see all factories.

## How long we keep it

Uploaded files and change records are kept permanently for audit purposes unless your organisation asks for them to be removed.

## Contact

Questions about this policy should go to your organisation's administrator.
